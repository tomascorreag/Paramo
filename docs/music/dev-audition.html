Paramo · bombo & flute audition

Every candidate below is already vendored and openly licensed — drums from uzu-drumkit (Unlicense), instruments from FluidR3 (MIT). Nothing here reaches a CDN.

Solo plays the layer alone on loop. In the mix plays the whole arrangement with that one layer swapped, which is the only way to judge whether a kick actually holds the bottom under the guitar. Both loop until you press stop or pick another.

(browsers block audio until you interact)

Bombo — the low kick on every beat

Current: sound("bd") index 0 at speed 0.9. speed is the main lever — below 1.0 it pitches the sample down and stretches it, which is what turns a drum-kit kick into something more like a struck bombo. Set it, then compare samples at that speed.

0.90 — lower = deeper & longer

Flute — the melody

Current: gm_pan_flute .n(1). All are FluidR3 GM programs, so the licence story is identical for every one — pick on sound alone. A huayno melody is idiomatically a quena (notched flute) or siku (panpipes), so the breathier programs are worth a listen even though the score says pan flute.
